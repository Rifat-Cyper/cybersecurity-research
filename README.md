## About This Portfolio

Welcome to my cybersecurity research portfolio. This repository documents academic research, literature reviews, technical analyses, and security investigation frameworks developed through graduate and doctoral-level study.

My interests focus on bridging cybersecurity research with practical security operations, including incident investigation, digital evidence handling, threat detection, security governance, and cloud identity management.

## Research Approach

My work emphasizes structured literature analysis, comparison of cybersecurity frameworks and technical approaches, identification of research limitations, and clear documentation of findings.

Where projects are conceptual or literature-based, this distinction is maintained to avoid presenting proposed techniques as experimentally validated results.
