# Pre-publication review checklist

- [ ] Check every citation against the cited source and DOI.
- [ ] Review unsupported technical or legal claims; avoid absolute guarantees about chain of custody, hashes or court admissibility.
- [ ] Confirm each item describes literature analysis rather than implying experiments were performed.
- [ ] Check original presentation for copyrighted graphics, course content, speaker notes and personal information.
- [ ] Approve the final course metadata (TIM-8310) and author attribution.
- [ ] Decide whether and how to license the work.
- [ ] Only then upload publicly to GitHub.

Note: The source Word files were transcribed into Markdown, not silently rewritten into new empirical studies.
